LAST WISH

Three heroes against Marionette NEO, Candlemaw or Deadeye

←↑↓→ or WASD move Z / Enter confirm, strike, shoot, jump X / Shift back, hold to focus while dodging M music and sound on or off

Getting started

  1. On the title screen, use Up/Down to pick a row and Left/Right to choose an enemy and a mode (Normal or Hard).
  2. Press Z to begin. Your first key press also starts the music.
  3. Each round, choose an action for each hero. Then survive the enemy's attack by steering your heart inside the green box.
  4. Win by bringing the enemy's HP to 0, or by raising its MERCY to 100% and sparing it.

Your turn

FIGHTA bar slides toward a mark. Press Z as it crosses. The closer you are, the more damage you do; dead center is a critical.
MAGICTerrestro's spells deal damage and Lune's spells heal. Spells cost EXP from the orange meter on the left.
ACTAstra's peaceful option. It raises the enemy's MERCY; how it works depends on the boss (see below).
ITEMA small shared bag of snacks. Heal any hero, including one who is down.
SPAREAny hero can SPARE. It only works once MERCY is 100% and the enemy's name has turned yellow.
DEFENDThat hero takes half damage this round and you gain 16% EXP.

EXP also comes from grazing: brush past bullets without touching them. A hero at 0 HP is down. They skip turns and slowly recover, or you can heal them sooner. If all three go down, it's game over.

The party

ASTRA · 150 HP

A galaxy-skinned star knight. Has ACT instead of MAGIC.

TERRESTRO · 200 HP

A cave monster and the hardest hitter. Damage spells: Crystal Spike (25% EXP) and Quake (55% EXP).

LUNE · 110 HP

A moon seer and healer. Moonbeam (15%), Mend (25%, can revive) and Aurora (65%, heals everyone).

Dodging

Your heart is the red icon inside the green box. Anything white hurts it. Some attacks change the rules:

  • BLUE bullets only hurt while you move. Stand still and let them pass.
  • ORANGE bullets only hurt while you stand still. Keep moving through them.
  • YELLOW HEART The heart flips upside down and Z fires shots upward. Yellow objects break when shot. Yellow TNT freezes in place, flashes a warning cross, then blasts its whole row and column. The blast only hurts you, so stay out of the lines!
  • BLUE HEART Gravity pulls the heart down. Tap Z for a short hop and hold Z for a full jump. You can land on platforms.
  • HAZARDS Some attacks add darkness, reversed controls, strings that drag you, wind, slowing wax puddles, or a shrinking box. The line under the box explains each one.

The bosses

MARIONETTE NEO

A scrap-metal puppet with 21 attacks. It turns frantic below half HP.

ACT: each turn's flavor text hints at its mood. Pick the matching ACT (Applaud, Dance, Oil Joints or Untangle) for +18% MERCY. Reveal shows the right answer next turn.

CANDLEMAW

A haunted candle with 12 attacks built on BLUE and ORANGE fire.

ACT: Keep Vigil always works: +8% MERCY every time.

DEADEYE

A ghost gunslinger. The first half uses the YELLOW HEART. At half HP or half MERCY it switches to the BLUE HEART.

ACT: Accept Duel gives +7% MERCY, but each use makes its attacks hit 5% harder.

Hard Mode

Pick HARD on the title screen. Enemies have more HP, hit harder, and attack at full fury from the first turn. Bullets are faster, the item bag is smaller, you recover less time after a hit, and MERCY rises more slowly.
